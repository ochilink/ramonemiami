About Ramone Walker

Ramone Walker has been a full-time Realtor since 2006 and is an active member of both the National Association of Realtors and the Miami Association of Realtors. Ramone currently serves as President on the board of Murano at Hampton Park and as a Director on the Economic Development Action Plan for the City of Miramar, Florida. He is also a past President of the Misty Lake Condominium Association.

Ramone’s daily job description entails being responsible for all day-to-day management decisions and for implementing long- and short-term plans. He holds a Bachelor's Degree in Business Administration from Florida International University, with a double major in Finance and International Business, as well as a Web Development Degree from Sheridan Technical College.

Since 2006, Ramone Walker has been helping buyers and sellers navigate the South Florida market—assisting clients to sell their homes, purchase new properties, invest in real estate, and manage their properties effectively.

Top Duties and Qualifications

Ramone Walker is responsible for overseeing real estate transactions between buyers and sellers. His core duties include drafting contracts, negotiating sale prices, and leading a team of Real Estate Agents to successfully close deals.

Working With Sellers

  • Listing homes for sale on the Multiple Listing Service (MLS).
  • Sharing brokerage listings with other MLS brokers to drive buyer traffic.
  • Advising home sellers on optimal preparation for listings and showings.
  • Supervising property showings and managing feedback.
  • Reporting sale results and buyer feedback directly to sellers.

Working With Buyers

  • Helping buyers locate properties within their desired geographical area, price range, and criteria.
  • Assisting buyers with scheduling and conducting property visits.
  • Guiding buyers through every stage of the sale transaction.
  • Delivering fast response times and excellent negotiating skills.
